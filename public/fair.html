🛡️ Provably fair

Don't trust us. Verify us.

Every Crash point, coin flip, and card shuffle on TrenchWars is locked in before you bet, in a way we can't change afterwards. You can check every result yourself.

How it works, in plain English

1
We lock in our secret first.
The server picks a random server seed and shows you only its fingerprint (a SHA-256 hash). The fingerprint proves we can't swap the seed later, but it doesn't let anyone predict results.
2
You add your own randomness.
Your client seed is yours. Change it any time in the Fairness tab. We can't know it in advance, so we can't pre-pick outcomes for you.
3
The blockchain adds randomness nobody controls.
When you place a bet, we record the latest finalized Solana blockhash (and its slot number) and mix it into your seed: clientSeed|blockhash. Neither you nor we can predict a future blockhash, and anyone can look up the slot on a Solana explorer.
4
Each bet gets a counter.
The nonce starts at 0 and goes up by 1 with every bet, so each bet gets a fresh result from the same seeds.
5
The result is pure math.
We compute HMAC-SHA256(serverSeed, clientSeed|blockhash:nonce) and turn the output into a game result with a public formula (below). Same inputs, same result, every time.
6
Reveal and verify.
Hit Rotate seed and we reveal the old server seed. Check that its SHA-256 matches the fingerprint you saw, then click Verify on any bet in your history. We recompute it in front of you, or you can run the code yourself.

The formulas

Coinflip: first byte of the HMAC. Even means heads, odd means tails.

Crash: if the first 8 hex chars mod 100 = 0, it crashes at 1.00× (that 1% is the house edge on free credits). Otherwise r = first 13 hex chars (52 bits) and crash = floor((100·2⁵² − r)/(2⁵² − r))/100. The curve rises as e^(0.00006·ms), and the server clock decides when you cashed out.

Blackjack: a 52-card deck shuffled with Fisher–Yates. Random numbers come from HMAC(serverSeed, clientSeed:nonce:cursor), 4 bytes at a time. Cards are dealt in order: you, dealer, you, dealer, then hits, then the dealer's draws.

// Node.js — verify a coinflip yourself
const c = require('crypto');
const h = c.createHmac('sha256', SERVER_SEED).update(`${CLIENT_SEED}|${BLOCKHASH}:${NONCE}`).digest('hex');
console.log(parseInt(h.slice(0,2),16) % 2 === 0 ? 'heads' : 'tails');
console.log(c.createHash('sha256').update(SERVER_SEED).digest('hex')); // must equal the hash you were shown

Bets placed while the Solana feed was unreachable have no blockhash recorded and use clientSeed:nonce. Full source: fair.js in the open-source repo.
