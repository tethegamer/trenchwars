Official Rules

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY. Credits have no cash value and can't be bought or withdrawn.
  1. Free to play. TrenchWars is a free entertainment game. There are no deposits, purchases, or paid entries of any kind. Buying anything never improves your chances.
  2. Credits. Every new account gets 1,000 free credits. Every player can claim free credits once every 24 hours. Claim on consecutive days (within 48 hours of your last claim) to build a streak: 500, 600, 700, 800, 1,000, 1,200, then 1,500 a day from day 7 on. Miss the window and the streak resets. You also get bonus credits for leveling up (1 XP per credit bet), for unlocking achievements, and for referrals: you and a new player who signs up with your invite link each get 500. Credits are virtual game points only. They have no monetary value and can't be bought, sold, traded, transferred, or withdrawn.
  3. Referrals. Referral bonuses are virtual credits only. Self-referral and fake accounts are prohibited and void all credits and rankings.
  4. Accounts. Sign in by signing a message with a Solana wallet (Phantom), which never sends a transaction, or use a username and password. One account per person. Multi-accounting, bots, or exploiting bugs means disqualification.
  5. Games. Crash, Coinflip (pays 1.96×), and Blackjack (dealer stands on all 17s, blackjack pays 3:2, double down on your first two cards, no splits or insurance). Outcomes are provably fair, and each bet also mixes in the latest finalized Solana blockhash as public randomness. See how it works.
  6. Weekly leaderboard. Players are ranked by net credits won (credits paid out minus credits wagered) from Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. The board resets every Monday.
  7. Prizes. Any prizes listed on the site are funded by the site owner and given away for free to the top-ranked eligible players when the week ends. Prizes, amounts, and the number of winners are set by the owner and shown in the Prizes section. Winners must have a connected Solana wallet to receive memecoin prizes. Prizes are not exchangeable for credits and credits are not exchangeable for prizes except through leaderboard rank.
  8. Eligibility. You must be 18+ (or the age of majority where you live), and the giveaway is void where prohibited. You are responsible for any taxes on prizes.
  9. Changes & disputes. The owner may change these rules, void results from bugs or abuse, or end the promotion at any time. The owner's decisions are final.
  10. No warranty. The service is provided as-is. Memecoins are volatile and the owner makes no representation about their value.

Provably fair

result = HMAC_SHA256(serverSeed, clientSeed + ":" + nonce). Before you play, you can see sha256(serverSeed). After you rotate, the seed is revealed so you can check every bet. Crash and Blackjack use the same stream (the deck is a Fisher–Yates shuffle). The exact code is in fair.js in the open-source repo.
